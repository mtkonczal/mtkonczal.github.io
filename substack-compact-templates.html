Four compact, horizontal cuts of the newsletter band

The current build runs ~630px tall on desktop — the widget card's own padding, header, and per-row dividers add up. Every option below keeps the same real, live-fetched post data but lays it out left-to-right instead of stacked, to see how much shorter it can go. Real posts, real pitch line (shortened per your note), nothing fabricated.

Option A
One row, everything inline
measured: 177px tall

Pitch phrase, a divider, three post titles side by side (truncated with an ellipsis if a title's too long to fit), then Subscribe on the far right. The single most compact option. Cost: long titles get cut off — "How I Use AI as a Policy Researcher…" loses its second half.

Option B
Pitch on top, 3-up strip below
measured: 202px tall

One line of pitch + the Subscribe button, then a thin three-column strip of the recent posts underneath, divided by rules like a mini table of contents. Best if: you want full titles to stay readable (they wrap to two lines instead of truncating) while still cutting the height by more than half.

Option C
Label line + headline ribbon
measured: 201px tall

The pitch reads as a plain sentence next to an eyebrow label rather than a standalone headline, so it takes less vertical room up top; the three posts become the visual focus, styled like headlines with a colored date tag above each. Best if: the posts themselves should do the selling, not the pitch copy.

Option D
Single ticker line
measured: 75px tall

Everything on one line: the pitch as a lead-in phrase, then the three post titles as plain links separated by hairlines, then Subscribe pinned to the right. Cost: the most cramped of the four — full titles genuinely don't fit next to a pitch phrase and a button in one line at most screen widths, so this one leans hardest on shortening the titles themselves, not just truncating them with CSS.
