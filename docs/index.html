Mike Konczal

Vice President of Policy and Research, Economic Security Project · Former White House chief economist

Mike Konczal is the Vice President of Policy and Research at the Economic Security Project, which works to build economic power for all Americans. Previously, he served as Special Assistant to the President for Economic Policy and Chief Economist at the National Economic Council.

A former software and financial engineer, he was an early hire at the Roosevelt Institute, where he led major projects on financial reform, inequality, economic ideas, and macroeconomics. He is the author of Freedom from the Market (New Press, 2021), described by the Financial Times as “a powerful polemic,” and co-author of Rewriting the Rules of the American Economy (W.W. Norton, 2015) with Joseph Stiglitz. He holds a B.A. in math and computer science and an M.S. in finance from the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign.

Specializing in economic data analysis, he writes and speaks widely on U.S. economic policy, is regularly cited in major national outlets, and has testified before Congress. The New York Times Magazine has described him as having “a cult following among progressives.” Born and raised in Chicago, he now lives in Takoma Park, Maryland, with his wife, two daughters, and pit bull.

The Affordability Framework
Current focus Economic Security Project, 2025
With Becky Chao, my current work at the Economic Security Project. It makes the case that the cost of living is the defining economic problem of our moment, and that fixing it means confronting both how markets and incomes are broken.
Supply-Side Expansion Has Driven the Decline in Inflation
Roosevelt Institute, 2023
I spent a lot of time in the trenches of the inflation debates. This shows that 2023's disinflation came from supply recovering rather than the economy weakening, the empirical core of the soft-landing case I argued inside and outside government.
Karl Polanyi for President
Dissent, 2016
Co-authored with Patrick Iber, tying the energy of the Sanders campaign to the idea of decommodification, the question of what we choose to keep out of the market. It led to my book and still works as a short introduction to my political project.
Rewriting the Rules of the American Economy
Roosevelt Institute, 2015
Co-authored with Joseph Stiglitz and others, this is the report that helped recast American inequality as the product of rules we write rather than forces we endure, and pushed the economic debates toward root causes.
Dodd-Frank Turns 5 Today - It's Obama's Most Underappreciated Achievement
Vox, 2015
My policy career began in financial reform in the wake of the 2008 crisis and Great Recession, work that fed into the Dodd-Frank Act. This is my later guide to what the law actually did.
